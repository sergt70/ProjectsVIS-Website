Data deletion requests

How to request deletion of connected-account data

Send a request from an email address reasonably connected to the relevant account or organization.

  1. Use the subject line “VIS Data Deletion Request”.
  2. Identify the connected platform.
  3. Provide the relevant account, channel or profile identifier.
  4. State whether you request full deletion or only disconnection.
  5. Provide information needed to verify your authority over the account.

Send the request to privacy@vis.example .

VIS may ask for limited additional information to protect the connected account and prevent fraudulent deletion requests.
