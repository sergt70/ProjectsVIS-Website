# VIS Global Market Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines how VIS prioritizes international markets for
future expansion.

The objective is to allocate content production and business development
toward markets with the strongest long-term commercial potential while
maintaining legal compliance and user trust.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Evaluation Principles

Every market should be evaluated using multiple criteria rather than
audience size alone.

Primary factors include:

-   Purchasing power
-   Digital advertising maturity
-   Business adoption
-   Subscription willingness
-   Platform availability
-   Long-term scalability

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Emerging Markets

Potential future expansion:

-   Latin America
-   Middle East
-   India
-   Southeast Asia

Each market should be evaluated independently before increasing
investment.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Content Localization

For every supported region VIS should consider:

-   Language
-   Cultural context
-   Platform preferences
-   Local regulations
-   Seasonal events
-   Business expectations

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Platform Strategy

The future platform should be able to:

-   support multiple languages
-   support multiple regions
-   select target markets
-   maintain localized metadata
-   compare market performance
-   learn from historical publishing results

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Long-Term Vision

VIS should evolve into an international platform capable of serving
creators, businesses and organizations across multiple countries while
maintaining one consistent architecture and brand identity.
