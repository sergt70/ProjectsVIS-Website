Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how VIS — Viral Intelligence System (“VIS”, “we”, “us” or “our”) may access, collect, use, store, protect and delete information when people visit the VIS website or voluntarily connect authorized social media accounts.

Effective date: August 6, 2026 Version: 1.0

Current project status: VIS is under development. The public website does not currently provide public registration or a public customer dashboard. Some functions described below apply only when an account owner voluntarily connects a supported social media account.

1. Scope of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the public VIS website and to VIS functions that use official authorization mechanisms to connect supported third-party platform accounts.

It is intended to cover accounts connected directly by their owners and accounts managed by a business or agency on behalf of a client who has granted the required authorization.

Third-party platforms operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Their independent processing of personal data is not controlled by VIS.

2. Who is responsible for processing

The operator of VIS is responsible for the processing activities described in this Privacy Policy, except where a third-party platform acts independently under its own policies.

The final legal name, registered address and official domain contact details will be added before VIS becomes publicly available to external customers.

3. Information we may process

3.1 Website information

The current public website is designed as a static informational website. It does not include advertising trackers, analytics services or public account registration at this stage.

The website may store a language preference in the visitor's browser using local storage. This preference is used only to remember the selected language and can be removed through the browser's site-data settings.

The hosting provider may automatically process ordinary technical request information, such as IP address, date and time, requested page, browser type, device information, referral information and security-related logs. Such processing may be necessary to deliver the website, maintain availability and protect it from abuse.

3.2 Information from authorized platform connections

Depending on the platform, the permissions approved by the account owner and the VIS function being used, VIS may process:

  • platform account or user identifiers;
  • basic profile information, such as profile name or channel name;
  • the list of OAuth permissions or scopes granted to VIS;
  • access tokens and, where provided, refresh tokens;
  • token status, issue time and expiration information;
  • content files and related publishing instructions submitted for publication;
  • titles, descriptions, captions, hashtags, privacy settings and other publishing metadata;
  • identifiers and status information returned after an upload or publication attempt;
  • technical records needed to diagnose errors, prevent duplicate publication and maintain security;
  • platform analytics or account data only where separately authorized and required for an enabled VIS function.

VIS seeks to request only permissions reasonably necessary for the functions being provided. Available data and permissions vary by platform.

4. OAuth connections and platform passwords

VIS uses official OAuth or equivalent authorization mechanisms offered by supported social media platforms. OAuth allows an account owner to grant specific permissions without giving VIS the password for that social media account.

VIS does not request, receive or store the user's TikTok, Google, YouTube or other connected-platform password.

A platform authorization screen identifies the requested permissions. The account owner decides whether to approve them. VIS uses an authorized connection only within the permissions granted by that owner and for the functions described in this Privacy Policy and the VIS Terms of Service.

4.1 TikTok

When TikTok is connected, VIS may receive a TikTok user identifier, basic profile information permitted by the approved scope, OAuth tokens and permissions required for authorized upload or posting functions. Content is sent only to an account whose owner has authorized the required scope.

4.2 Google and YouTube

When Google or YouTube is connected, VIS may receive channel or account identifiers, authorized OAuth tokens, granted scopes and information required to perform the selected YouTube API function, such as uploading a video or retrieving the result of an authorized publishing request.

VIS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

4.3 Other platforms

If VIS later supports additional platforms, this Privacy Policy will be updated where necessary to describe the relevant categories of data, permissions and purposes before those integrations are made available to users.

5. How we use information

VIS may use the information described above to:

  • identify and display the voluntarily connected account;
  • maintain the authorized connection;
  • prepare content and platform-specific publishing metadata;
  • upload or publish content requested by the authorized account owner or operator;
  • retrieve and display the status or result of a publishing operation;
  • prevent accidental duplicate publications;
  • keep necessary operational and security records;
  • diagnose errors and provide support;
  • protect VIS, connected accounts and users from misuse or unauthorized access;
  • comply with applicable law and enforce VIS terms and policies;
  • improve VIS functions using permitted operational information.

VIS does not use connected-account information to make guaranteed claims about views, reach, followers, revenue or platform approval.

6. Legal bases for processing

Where applicable data-protection law requires a legal basis, VIS may rely on one or more of the following:

  • Contractual necessity: processing needed to provide a requested VIS function.
  • Consent: where the user voluntarily grants platform permissions or where consent is otherwise required.
  • Legitimate interests: operating, securing, troubleshooting and improving VIS, provided those interests are not overridden by individual rights.
  • Legal obligation: processing required to comply with applicable law or lawful requests.

Granting OAuth permission does not prevent the account owner from later revoking platform access.

7. Sharing and service providers

VIS does not sell personal data.

Information may be transmitted to or processed by:

  • the social media platform selected by the user, in order to complete an authorized account or publishing operation;
  • infrastructure, hosting, security, storage or communications providers that support operation of VIS;
  • professional advisers where reasonably necessary and subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations;
  • public authorities where disclosure is required by applicable law, valid legal process or a lawful request;
  • a successor organization in connection with a legitimate restructuring, acquisition or transfer, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

Service providers are expected to process information only for authorized purposes and with appropriate confidentiality and security protections.

8. Storage and security

VIS applies technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss or destruction.

Measures may include:

  • encryption of stored OAuth tokens;
  • restricted access to credentials and operational data;
  • separation between the public website and the internal VIS system;
  • secure transport using HTTPS in production;
  • logging and monitoring of relevant security events;
  • backup, recovery and access-control procedures where appropriate;
  • periodic review of permissions and connected integrations.

No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. VIS therefore cannot promise absolute security, but it is designed to use protections appropriate to the nature of the information processed.

9. Data retention

VIS retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including to maintain a requested connection, complete publishing operations, provide support, protect against duplicate actions, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes.

OAuth tokens are deleted or rendered unusable when the connection is removed, the user requests deletion, the token is revoked, or retention is no longer necessary, subject to limited legal, security and backup requirements.

Technical logs may be retained for a limited period appropriate to security, troubleshooting and audit needs. The specific retention schedule will be documented before VIS is offered broadly to external customers.

10. Disconnecting an account and revoking access

An account owner may stop VIS access by:

  • using an available disconnect function within VIS;
  • revoking VIS access from the connected platform's account or security settings;
  • sending an account-disconnection or data-deletion request to the contact address below.

Revoking platform access prevents future API access through the revoked authorization. It may not automatically remove records that VIS must retain for legitimate security, legal or dispute purposes.

Google users can review and remove third-party account access in their Google Account security settings. TikTok users can manage connected-app permissions through the settings provided by TikTok.

11. Privacy rights and data requests

Depending on location and applicable law, an individual may have rights to:

  • receive information about the processing of personal data;
  • request access to personal data held by VIS;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • request deletion of personal data in applicable circumstances;
  • request restriction of certain processing;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • receive certain information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where processing relies on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection authority.

To protect connected accounts and personal information, VIS may need to verify the requester's identity and authority over the relevant account before completing a request.

Requests will be handled within the timeframe required by applicable law. Some rights may be subject to lawful exceptions.

12. Children's privacy

VIS is not directed to children and is not intended to be used by anyone who is not legally permitted to authorize the relevant account or service in their jurisdiction.

VIS does not knowingly seek to collect personal data from children through the public website. If we learn that such information was collected inappropriately, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

13. International processing

VIS and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where the user is located. Data-protection laws may differ between jurisdictions.

Where required, VIS will use appropriate safeguards for international transfers, such as an adequacy mechanism, contractual safeguards or another transfer method recognized by applicable law.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when VIS functions, integrations, legal requirements or data practices change.

The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through an appropriate additional notice where required.

15. Contact and data-deletion requests

For privacy questions, account-disconnection requests, access requests or deletion requests, contact:
