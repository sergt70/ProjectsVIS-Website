OAuth and data protection

Accounts remain under the control of their owners

VIS uses official OAuth authorization mechanisms provided by supported social platforms. Access is limited to permissions approved by the account owner.

No social password storage VIS does not request or store account passwords for connected social media services.
Permission-based access Functions are performed only within scopes approved during OAuth authorization.
Encrypted token storage OAuth tokens used by VIS are stored in encrypted form.
Revocable connections Account owners can revoke platform access or request disconnection and deletion of related data.
Important: VIS does not sell personal data and does not promise guaranteed views, reach, followers, revenue or platform approval.
